Instructions for the LineSnap User Study:

In this study, you will be shown 50 different line charts from online news sources, such as the New York Times & the Wall Street Journal. For each visualization, you will answer one question designed to understand how you identify patterns in line charts.


For each visualization, you will be asked: "What pattern do you see in the line chart?"

There are 7 choices, each representing a line chart pattern. If the pattern does not align with the 7 given patterns, please select "Other" and type in your answer.


You will have 5 seconds for each question. There is a static screen for 2 seconds between each question to clear the previous visual stimuli. Please answer as best as you can. Your honest answers will help us understand how people identify line chart patterns in real-world contexts.


When you're ready to begin, click "Start Study" below. The study should take approximately 15-20 minutes to complete.
