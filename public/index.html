LineSnap User Study: Identifying Line Chart Patterns

The purpose of this research study is to learn how people identify line charts in public domain articles.


Being in a research study is completely voluntary. You can choose not to be in this research study. You can also say yes now and change your mind later. Note that if you end the study before completing it, your data will not be recorded.


If you agree to take part in this research, you will be shown a series of charts and asked to use those charts to estimate specific statistics or patterns. Your participation in this study will take less than 30 minutes. You can choose to stop taking the survey at any time. You must be at least 18 years old to participate. If you are younger than 18 years old, please do not proceed with this study.


Compensation: Participants will be entered into a raffle for a chance to win a $50 gift card upon completion of the study.


Please complete this study using a laptop or desktop computer, not a mobile phone or tablet.


The possible risks to you in taking part in this research are: Consequences of breach of confidentiality. The possible benefits to you for taking part in this research are: There are no direct benefits to subjects as a result of participating in this study.
