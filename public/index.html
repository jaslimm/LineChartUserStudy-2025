Studying Patterns in Line Charts

The purpose of this research study is to understand what patterns people identify in line charts, particularly in public domains, such as digital media.


Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to participate or to withdraw at any time without penalty. However, please note that if you exit the study before completing it, your responses will not be recorded.


If you agree to take part, you will view a series of 50 line charts and answer brief multiple-choice questions about the patterns you identify. The study will take less than 20 minutes to complete. You must be at least 18 years old to participate. If you are under 18, please do not proceed with this study. Please complete this study on a laptop or desktop computer. Mobile phones and tablets are not supported.


Compensation: Upon successful completion of the study, you will be automatically entered into a raffle for a chance to win a $50 gift card. If the study is incomplete or did not pass the all of the attention checks, then you will not be entered into the raffle.


There are no expected risks beyond those of everyday online activity, although a potential risk could include loss of confidentiality. There are no direct personal benefits from participating, but your responses will contribute to a better understanding of how people interpret data visualizations.
