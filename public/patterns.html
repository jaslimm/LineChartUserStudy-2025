Patterns

In this study, you will view 50 different line charts, each accompanied by one task designed to identify the patterns you see. The study focuses on the following 8 chart patterns in line charts:


Upward/Increasing: Values steadily increase over time, showing a general trend of growth.


Downward/Decreasing: Values steadily decrease over time, showing a general trend of decline.


Peak/Spike: Values rise to a single high point.


Valley/Dip: Values drop to a single low point.


Periodic: Values rise and fall repeatedly in a regular, recognizable pattern.


Uniform: Values stay mostly the same over time with little noticeable change.


Irregular: Values fluctuate randomly without a clear pattern or direction.


Other: Patterns that do not align with the prior 7 patterns.
